# Ryan Corp Lead Response Setup — Pilot Offer
Status: sales offer draft. Only sell after the stated deliverables are implemented and tested.

## The offer

**Capture more website enquiries without making your team learn another complicated CRM.**

Ryan Corp configures a simple website assistant that answers common questions from information your business approves, collects agreed enquiry details and routes requests to your team.

## Who it is for

Independent auto repair shops and small local service businesses with:
- an active website;
- recurring service, quote or appointment enquiries;
- a named person who handles new leads; and
- approved business information to power the assistant.

## Pilot scope

- One website assistant on one website.
- Up to 30 approved FAQs / service facts supplied by the customer.
- Enquiry capture using agreed fields.
- One agreed lead destination (secure inbox or tested email notification).
- Human handoff for unknown or sensitive questions.
- Basic test report and one adjustment round.
- One month of limited pilot support.

## Pilot price hypothesis

$99 one-time setup + $39 for the first month.

This is a proposed validation price, not a confirmed market rate. Final price depends on delivery cost, AI usage, hosting, customer requirements and payment fees. No unlimited messages or unsupported integrations are included.

## What is not included

- WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger or SMS automation.
- Voice answering or outbound calling.
- Confirmed calendar bookings.
- CRM, inventory or payment integrations.
- Guaranteed leads, sales or conversion lift.
- Legal, medical, financial or emergency advice.
- Unlimited AI usage or 24/7 human support.

Anything outside the written scope requires a separate feasibility check and quote.

## Customer onboarding checklist

1. Confirm business identity, website ownership/authorization and primary contact.
2. Agree written scope, price, pilot period and acceptance criteria.
3. Collect approved business facts: services, hours, service area, public contact details, FAQs and escalation contact.
4. Agree exactly which lead fields are necessary and why.
5. Approve privacy notice, retention period and lead destination.
6. Configure a test environment with fictional data first.
7. Test at least 20 expected questions, unknown questions, invalid form entries, abuse attempts and mobile layout.
8. Customer reviews the answers and signs off.
9. Enable production only after security, data storage, notification and deletion checks pass.
10. Review results after 7 days and at the end of the pilot.

## Acceptance criteria

A pilot is ready only when:
- answers are grounded in customer-approved information;
- unknown questions clearly route to a human;
- lead submissions are actually stored and delivered to the agreed destination;
- unauthorized users cannot view another customer's leads;
- the customer can request export and deletion;
- rate limits and basic abuse protection are active;
- mobile and desktop tests pass; and
- customer approves the final test report.

## Sales qualification questions

- Where do new enquiries arrive today?
- Who responds, and how quickly during and after business hours?
- Which questions are repeated most often?
- What information must be collected before staff can respond?
- Where should an enquiry be delivered?
- What would make a 30-day pilot worthwhile?
- What customer data should never be collected?

## Internal rule

Never imply the current static demo is a live customer-ready system. Do not accept payment or real customer data until every acceptance criterion is met and a lawful payment route is verified.
