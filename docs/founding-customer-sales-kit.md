# ChatBoost AI — founding-customer sales kit

## What we sell today

A guided pilot that helps a small business respond to common customer enquiries more consistently and keep track of interested prospects.

The current public app is an interactive preview, not a production multi-tenant SaaS. Do not claim that it already connects to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, autonomously sends messages, stores customer records in secure accounts, or processes payments. Those capabilities require implementation, provider approval, testing and customer-specific configuration.

## Best first customer profiles

Prioritize owner-managed businesses that receive repeated questions about prices, availability, bookings, stock, delivery or property listings:

1. Real-estate agencies — repeated listing, price, location and viewing enquiries.
2. Beauty salons and spas — service menus, prices, appointment and opening-hour questions.
3. Independent car dealers and rental firms — vehicle availability, specifications, viewing and booking enquiries.
4. Restaurants and caterers — menu, hours, location, delivery and reservation questions.

Start with one business at a time. Ask how enquiries arrive and what questions they answer repeatedly. Do not assume the business has a response-time or lost-sales problem; verify it in the discovery call.

## Founding-customer pilot offer (pricing hypothesis)

- Pilot setup: 15,000 XAF one-time.
- Includes: one discovery session, a business-specific FAQ/reply library, up to 20 approved reply templates, a simple lead-follow-up tracker, and a walkthrough.
- Optional monthly support after the pilot: 10,000 XAF/month, only if the customer agrees after seeing the deliverables.
- No automatic messaging or channel integration is included in this pilot.
- Any AI API usage, paid hosting, messaging-provider fees or third-party subscription must be disclosed and approved before being incurred.

Treat these as test prices, not guaranteed market rates. Offer a written scope and receipt. Do not promise a sales increase or guaranteed revenue.

## Discovery questions

1. Where do most enquiries arrive: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, calls, or walk-ins?
2. What are the five questions customers ask most often?
3. Who currently replies, and what happens when they are busy?
4. How do you remember to follow up with someone who asked for a price but did not buy?
5. What would make a one-week pilot worth paying for?

## Follow-up (send once, after 3–5 business days)

Hello [Name], just following up on my note about ChatBoost AI. If responding to repeated enquiries or remembering follow-ups is a challenge for your team, I can show you a short example using your public business information. If it's not a priority, no problem and I won't keep messaging.

## Demo checklist

- Use only public business information or information the owner explicitly supplies.
- Clearly label AI-generated replies as drafts for staff approval.
- Never invent prices, availability, policies, delivery times or guarantees.
- Do not upload private customer conversations or personal data without a lawful basis and the business's explicit authorization.
- Get approval before publishing or sending any customer-facing message.
- Explain exactly what is manual in the pilot and what is not yet automated.

## Prospecting workflow

1. Select 10 businesses from public business directories or map listings.
2. Check their official website or public social profile for the correct contact route.
3. Personalize the first sentence based on their actual services; do not claim to have audited their inbox.
4. Contact the public business number, email or business page once.
5. Record date, channel, response and next action in a lead tracker.
6. Stop if they decline or ask not to be contacted.
7. Never buy scraped personal contact lists or send bulk unsolicited messages.

## Product readiness gates before a full SaaS launch

- Secure authentication and separate workspaces for each business.
- Tenant-scoped database access and authorization tests.
- Persistent contacts, products, FAQs and follow-up records.
- Server-side AI integration with rate limits, cost controls and clear usage limits.
- Official Meta/WhatsApp integrations with required permissions and review.
- Privacy notice, terms, data deletion/export and retention process.
- Payment-provider eligibility, settlement and webhook verification.
- Monitoring, backups, automated tests and security review.

Until these are complete, sell only the defined guided pilot and the working features actually demonstrated.
