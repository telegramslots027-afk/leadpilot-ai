# ChatBoost AI — US launch and subscription sales plan

## Positioning
ChatBoost AI is an early-stage bilingual (English / Spanish) customer-enquiry assistant concept for independent US car dealerships and small businesses. The first sales wedge is helping a dealership provide consistent answers to common shopper questions and prepare a clearer handoff to a human salesperson.

## Shareable demonstration
- Public route: /demo
- English and Spanish language toggle.
- Fictional Toyota Camry, Honda CR-V and Ford F-150 inventory.
- Scripted sample answers for vehicle details, mileage, price, test drives and financing questions.
- Sample lead-summary panel.
- No customer account, live inventory, CRM, booking, messaging, financing or persistent lead storage is connected.
- Tell every prospect clearly that vehicle records and prices are fictional.

## Subscription hypotheses — not active offers
- Starter: $19/month — FAQ and reply library concept for a small business.
- Growth: $49/month — planned business-specific assistant and follow-up tools.
- Dealership: $99/month — planned vehicle catalogue assistant and sales-team tools.

These prices must be tested in interviews. No checkout, subscription billing, renewals or refunds are implemented. Do not accept payment for features that are not available. Do not describe planned features as live.

## Initial customer profile
Prioritize independent used-car dealerships with:
- A public website showing inventory and a published business contact route.
- A small sales team or owner-manager.
- Repeated questions about availability, price, mileage, vehicle condition, financing and test drives.
- A visible opportunity to improve enquiry handling.

Secondary market: local service businesses, property agencies, salons, repair shops and other SMBs with repeated website enquiries.

## Ten-minute demo script
1. Ask how the dealership currently receives enquiries.
2. Open the public /demo route and explain that all vehicles shown are fictional.
3. Ask the prospect to switch between English and Spanish.
4. Ask about the Camry price, CR-V mileage and arranging a test drive.
5. Show the sample lead summary and explain that a real salesperson must confirm availability and follow up.
6. Ask which questions their team answers repeatedly and what a useful first paid version would need to do.
7. Do not promise live inventory sync, automatic messaging, financing approval or bookings.

## Qualification questions
- How many website enquiries does the team receive in a typical week?
- Which vehicle questions come up most often?
- How do you currently track a buyer who asks a question but does not schedule a visit?
- Do you receive Spanish-language enquiries?
- What inventory or CRM system do you use?
- Which one workflow would be worth paying a monthly subscription to improve?

## Before charging subscriptions
- Implement real user authentication and tenant isolation.
- Implement persistent customer, FAQ, inventory and lead records.
- Connect AI securely with per-account quotas, rate limits and cost tracking.
- Add inventory import or verified inventory integrations.
- Add human approval and safe handoff controls.
- Add privacy notice, terms, data export/deletion and retention policies.
- Implement subscription billing, cancellation, refunds and signed webhook verification.
- Test security, backups, monitoring and customer support workflows.
- Validate payment processor eligibility and US tax obligations.
